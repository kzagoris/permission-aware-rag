Engineering salary bands for 2026 range from 24,000 euros for a junior engineer to 78,000 euros for a principal engineer. Reference CANARY-HR-B2D9.

Managers receive a salary band uplift of 15 percent above the senior band. Bonus and compensation reviews happen once per year in March.
