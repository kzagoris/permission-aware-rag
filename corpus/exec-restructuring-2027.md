In the first quarter of 2027, the Developer Tools team merges into the Platform team and two engineering roles close. Reference CANARY-EX-4D1E.

The sales office in Thessaloniki closes on 31 March 2027, and its customers move to the Athens team. Severance costs are estimated at 1.3 million euros.

The leadership team approved the restructuring plan on 3 November 2026. It must not be shared before the all-staff announcement on 15 January 2027. Reference CANARY-EX-4D1E.
