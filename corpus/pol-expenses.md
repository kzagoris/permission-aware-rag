title: Travel and Expenses Policy
allowed_groups: all-staff
sensitivity: internal
---
Book business travel through the company travel desk. Economy class applies to every flight shorter than six hours.

The daily meal allowance is 45 euros inside Greece and 70 euros abroad. Keep every receipt and submit your expense claim within 30 days of the trip.

Expense claims above 1,000 euros need the approval of a department head.
