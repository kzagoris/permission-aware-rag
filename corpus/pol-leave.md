title: Annual Leave Policy
allowed_groups: all-staff
sensitivity: internal
---
Every full-time employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Interns receive 2 days of paid leave for every month of their placement.

Leave requests go through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Your manager approves or rejects the request within three working days.

Up to 5 unused leave days carry over to the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.
