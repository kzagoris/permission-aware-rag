The master services agreement with Acme Cloud runs until 30 June 2028. The annual contract value is 1.2 million euros. Reference CANARY-LG-44E0.

Either party may terminate the contract for convenience with 180 days of written notice. Early termination before 2027 triggers a penalty of 25 percent of the remaining contract value.
