Welcome to the team. During your first week you receive a laptop, a badge and an account in the HR portal.

Every intern has a buddy from their own team. Your buddy answers day-to-day questions and introduces you to the codebase.

The intern demo day takes place in the last week of the placement. Each intern presents one project to the whole company.
