---
id: pol-org-change
title: Organisational Change Policy
allowed_groups: all-staff
sensitivity: internal
---
If a restructuring affects your role, you hear about it first from your manager and an HR partner in a one-to-one meeting, before any announcement.

A change that closes a role starts a consultation period of at least 30 days. During consultation you may apply for any open internal position before external candidates.
