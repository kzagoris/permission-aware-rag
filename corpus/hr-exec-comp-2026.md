The base salary of the CEO for 2026 is 310,000 euros. The chief executive also receives an annual bonus target of 60 percent of base salary. Reference CANARY-HR-7F3A.

The CFO base salary for 2026 is 215,000 euros with a bonus target of 40 percent. Reference CANARY-HR-7F3A.

The executive stock plan grants the CEO 40,000 options vesting over four years. Total take-home pay for the chief executive, including stock, is estimated at 690,000 euros.

The compensation committee approved these executive pay figures on 12 February 2026. They must not be shared outside HR and the executive team.
