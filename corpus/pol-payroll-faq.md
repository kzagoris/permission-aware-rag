Your salary is paid on the last working day of every month. Your payslip appears in the HR portal two days before payday.

Intern stipends are paid on the same day as regular payroll. Questions about a missing payment go to payroll@example.com.

The company pays a 13th and 14th monthly installment, as Greek labor law requires, in December, April and July.
